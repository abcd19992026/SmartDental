The Problem

Why Manual Follow-up Registers Miss Patients

Manual reminder diaries fail because staff get busy. SmartDentist sends the reminders automatically, based on the treatment recorded at the visit.

❌ The Old Manual Way

  • ✕ Phone Calls Ignored: Patients don't answer unknown clinic phone calls while at work or driving.
  • ✕ Paper Registers & Lost Records: Physical case cards get misplaced, torn, or ruined with tea stains.
  • ✕ Empty Chair Hours: Inconsistent appointment booking creates dry clinic days with zero revenue.

✅ The SmartDentist Way

  • ✓ Automatic Recall Dates: Every treatment type has its own recall interval, set by your clinic. The due date is set as soon as the visit is recorded.
  • ✓ WhatsApp Delivery: Reminders go straight to the patient's WhatsApp, so nobody has to dial a number.
  • ✓ Records & Tooth Chart: Visit and treatment history, tooth chart, prescriptions and payments are all on the patient's record.
  • ✓ Replies Come Back to You: When a patient replies on WhatsApp, it shows up under “Replies Waiting” for your staff to act on.
Simple 3-Step Process

How Automated Recalls Work

No extra work for your front desk. Follow-ups run straight from your standard chairside workflow.

01

Record the Treatment

When you add the visit, pick the treatment done (e.g. Scaling / Cleaning, Filling, RCT, Extraction) and tap the teeth treated on the tooth chart.

02

Recall Date Sets Automatically

SmartDentist sets the recall date using the interval your clinic has set for that treatment type. Each treatment starts with a suggested interval that you can change.

03

Patient Is Reminded on WhatsApp

A WhatsApp reminder goes out the day before the due date and a follow-up the day after. If an appointment time is set, another goes out 2 hours before it. Patient replies show up for your staff under “Replies Waiting”.

Automatic WhatsApp Recall Reminders

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up SmartDentist for your clinic.

How does the automated WhatsApp recall reminder work?

When you record a treatment for a patient (like Scaling, RCT, Extraction, or Ortho), SmartDentist sets the recall date using the recall interval your clinic has set for that treatment type. The patient gets a WhatsApp reminder the day before the due date and a follow-up the day after, plus one 2 hours before the appointment if a time is set, without your staff having to dial a single number. If the patient replies, the reply appears under “Replies Waiting”.

Do I need to install any heavy software or buy new computers?

No! SmartDentist runs 100% in your web browser. You can access it on your existing clinic laptop, desktop, tablet, or smartphone. There are zero installation files or complicated server setups.

Can my receptionist use it too?

Yes. There are two kinds of login: the clinic owner, who sees every branch, and receptionists, who each see only their own branch's patients. Receptionists can register patients, book appointments, check patients in and record payments. Settings and the recall report are for the owner only.

Can we set our own recall intervals?

Yes. Each treatment type has its own recall interval in days, which you can change in Settings. New clinics start with suggested intervals (for example 180 days after scaling, 7 days after an extraction), and you can add your own treatment types. Reminders are sent in English using a WhatsApp message template approved by Meta.

Is our patient data safe and confidential?

Your data is stored in a cloud database that is encrypted in transit and at rest. Database access rules mean each clinic can only see its own records, and receptionists only their own branch's patients. A patient's name and number are sent to WhatsApp only to deliver that patient's messages.
